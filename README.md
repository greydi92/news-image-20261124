## The day's stories

1. **Air-raid alert on Ulleung Island as jets scramble** — South Korea
   briefly issues an evacuation warning for Ulleung Island on Tuesday
   morning after aircraft enter the Korea Air Defense Identification
   Zone; fighter jets are scrambled, residents are told to shelter,
   and the alert is lifted within minutes. Sources: Reuters, AP,
   Yonhap.
2. **Gukesh vs Sindarov: world chess title match begins in Geneva** —
   Game 1 of the FIDE World Championship Match is played on Tuesday
   24 November at the Fondation Martin Bodmer in Cologny, Geneva,
   defending champion Gukesh Dommaraju against challenger Javokhir
   Sindarov, for a $2.5 million prize fund. Sources: FIDE, Chess.com.
3. **North Korea threatens to shut the Swiss office in Pyongyang** —
   Pyongyang condemns Switzerland over sanctions and says, via KCNA,
   it will consider shutting down the Swiss diplomatic office in
   Pyongyang, barely a month after it reopened. Sources: Reuters,
   KCNA.
4. **Beaver supermoon meets the Pleiades** — the full Beaver Moon
   reaches its peak at 14:53 UTC on Tuesday 24 November, a supermoon
   passing close to the Pleiades star cluster in Taurus. Sources:
   EarthSky, NASA skywatching guide, U.S. Naval Observatory.

Also today: the Saryu-Kamala Express reaches Janakpur Dham at 10 a.m.
on Tuesday 24 November — the first direct India–Nepal passenger train,
flagged off the day before by PM Narendra Modi and Nepal PM Balendra
Shah. Sources: Indian Railways timetable reporting, The Daily Jagran.

## How it works

- `entry.png` (1024×1024 JPEG, ≤1 MiB) is the image, generated in-session
  for this entry — no photos, no downloaded images, no borrowed artwork.
  Four illustrated panels (no text inside the illustrations) with
  headline captions drawn on top, a masthead, a red date banner and a
  footer strip.
- `main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only, no network) copies the shipped
  `entry.png` to `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (or `/output`), so two runs write
  byte-identical output.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
